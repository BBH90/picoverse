PICO TI SALUTA — COPIONE DI REGIA (versione 3)
================================================
Regola d'oro: NESSUN TAGLIO NETTO e NESSUNA DISSOLVENZA tra un mondo e l'altro. Ogni passaggio e' una clip
"ponte" generata con PRIMO e ULTIMO fotogramma, cosi' l'azione continua senza stacchi.
Due mondi: CAMERA = pagina del libro colorata (stile pastello) · FUORI/SPAZIO = stile "dipinto" del montaggio.
Si esce dal libro dalla finestra (C2c) e ci si rientra dalla finestra (C7c).
Nei prompt MAI la parola 'axolotl' (lo fa diventare ROSA).

C0 — La pagina prende vita · INIZIO PAGINA29_BN.png · ULTIMO CAMERA_COLORI.png
A black and white coloring book page magically comes to life; static camera. First the little stars on the wall start to twinkle and turn golden, then soft colors flow out of them like watercolor paint filling the lines, as if painted by an invisible brush: the wall becomes a pastel gradient from sky blue to lilac and peach with soft clouds, the blanket turns deep blue with golden stars, the bed and the dresser turn light wood, the helmet on the dresser shines white and blue, the window fills with a starry night with a crescent moon and snowflakes. As the color reaches the little sleeping character, his cheeks blush first, then his leaf-shaped gills turn coral and his skin becomes pale mint green. He keeps sleeping peacefully with a gentle smile and does not move. Tiny golden sparkles float in the air. Smooth continuous magical transformation, no cuts, no text.

C1 — Sveglia · INIZIO CAMERA_COLORI.png
Static camera; the pastel bedroom never changes. The little mint-green cartoon character Pico, with coral leaf-shaped gills and rosy cheeks, sleeps in his bed under the deep blue blanket with golden stars. His gills wiggle, he opens one eye, then the other, rubs his eyes with his tiny hands and gives a big yawn. He stretches his arms up high and sits up in bed. Outside the window on the right, one golden star blinks twice as if winking at him; Pico gasps with joy, his gills flutter happily and he claps his little hands, looking at the window with a big smile. Only Pico and the star move; the helmet stays on the dresser. Keep the exact same character design, colors and soft pastel cartoon style. Smooth continuous motion, no cuts, no morphing, no text.
